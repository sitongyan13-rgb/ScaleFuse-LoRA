# Upstream software and data

ScaleFuse-LoRA is implemented as an extension to MMDetection's Grounding DINO model.

- Grounding DINO: https://github.com/IDEA-Research/GroundingDINO
- MMDetection: https://github.com/open-mmlab/mmdetection
- MMEngine: https://github.com/open-mmlab/mmengine
- MMCV: https://github.com/open-mmlab/mmcv
- ODinW protocol and datasets: https://github.com/microsoft/GLIP

The upstream projects and datasets retain their own licenses, notices, attribution requirements, and terms of use. Pretrained weights and datasets are not redistributed in this repository.
